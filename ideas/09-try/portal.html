Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Select Try it and the Portal opens as a guest with a virtual balance. We only ask for an email if you want to save your progress or open a real account.

Is the demo using real money?

No. The balance, the prices and the trades in the demo are virtual and simulated. Nothing you do in the demo can make or lose real money.

What happens to my progress if I register?

Your guest session is handed over to your new account, so you keep what you set up and start from the same screen. Your real balance always starts from what you deposit.

Is the Portal different from the app?

The Portal is the web app. You can use it in a browser or add it to your Home Screen. It uses the same account and login as the XMTrading app.
