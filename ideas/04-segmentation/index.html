One cookie.
Six first impressions.

A first-party cookie remembers what the visitor came for. The homepage, the registration form and the next step then speak to that intent. Prices, required fields and legal text never change. Clients who come back get a short, secure sign-in instead of a sales page.

      Where the line is
      
    Adapt the message and the helpers. Never the rules.
    
      
    Segmentation changes what we say and what we pre-fill, so the form feels made for the visitor. It never changes what is required, what is charged or what is disclosed. That keeps it safe to run, and easy for Compliance to sign off.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Adapts per visitor
    
        
      
          
    • Two homepage blocksThe hero visual and the link under the button. The hero headline, subline, button and background stay as today.
      • 
          
    • Registration and next-step copyHeadline, subline and button label on the registration page and the next step.
      • 
          
    • One helper row on the registration formPre-selected account type, platform note, practice account, or a chat shortcut.
      • 
          
    • Partner code fieldFilled from the cookie, so there is nothing to type.
      • 
          
    • Next-step orderWhat to do first after verifying.
      • 
          
    • Language and helpLocal-language chat and support when the cookie says so.
      • 
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Never adapts
    
        
      
          
    • Required fields and validationCountry, email and password, plus every KYC and entity check.
      • 
          
    • Terms, risk warning and consentsLegal text is identical for everyone and in the same place.
      • 
          
    • Prices, spreads and conditionsNo segment sees a different cost. Offers need Compliance approval before they are shown.
      • 
          
    • Regulated entity and region rulesDecided by country of residence, not by the cookie.
      • 
          
    • Anything the visitor did not chooseThe cookie holds a segment label only: no name, email or behaviour log.

      Why it pays
      
    Message match lifts registration, segment by segment.
    
      
    Illustrative lift in registration rate against today's one-size-fits-all page. Control = 1.0×.
    
      
    
      
    IllustrativeNot measured. For new visitors this is registration rate; for returning clients it is login-to-trade rate.
    
    
    
    
    
      How it would ship
      
    Start with what we already know.
    
      
    
        
    Phase 1 · XMTrading-side signalsNo partner work neededLanding page and campaign URL, affiliate ID, referrer, the xmtrading.com pages they browse next, language, country and device, plus a returning-client marker. The cookie is written on arrival and the pages read it.
    
        
    Phase 2 · What they readNeeds partner buy-inAffiliates pass the topic of the page the visitor read, as a landing parameter or a light tag. Only worth building once Phase 1 shows lift.
    
      
    
      
      
        
    1. XMTrading writes the xm_seg cookieFirst-party, 90 days, segment label only. Rules above, evaluated once at the edge. Consent wording to be confirmed with Legal.
      2. 
        
    2. Pages read the personaHomepage blocks (hero visual and link), the registration copy and helper row, and the next step come from one config, so marketing can edit copy without a release.
      3. 
      
    
      
    Guardrail: keep a 10% control hold-out so the lift is measured, not assumed. Anyone without the cookie, or below the 0.60 score, sees the page they see today.
