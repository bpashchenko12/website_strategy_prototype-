Questions

Terms in plain language.

Who can enter?

Existing clients whose last trade was at least 90 days ago. Illustrative rule for this prototype; final eligibility to be confirmed by Compliance.

How is the 50% bonus calculated?

Illustrative: 50% of your qualifying deposit, up to $500 in total. For example a $400 deposit would earn a $200 bonus. A bonus is not withdrawable until the trading-volume requirement is met.

What does XMP ×2 for 60 days mean?

Illustrative alternative: instead of a deposit bonus, your XMP loyalty points accrue at double the normal rate for 60 days after you enter.

What is the “offer number”?

Each campaign is capped at 5,000 entries. Your number shows your place in the series, for example #1,284 of 5,000, and is confirmed when you log in.

Can I combine this with other promotions?

Illustrative: one welcome-back offer per client per campaign. It cannot be combined with another deposit bonus on the same deposit.

What happens after 30 June?

Entries close at 23:59:59 on the end date and unclaimed numbers return to the pool. The next campaign in the series is announced by email and on the Portal.
