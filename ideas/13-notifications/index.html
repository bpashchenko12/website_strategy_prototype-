USD/JPY holds near 157 as traders weigh Bank of Japan signals

XR
XM ResearchUpdated 5 Nov 2026 · 6 min read
USD/JPY · 4H157.156-0.61%1H4H1D
Illustrative chart. Figures are not live prices.

The yen steadied against the dollar this week as markets priced a narrower rate gap, but the pair remains within sight of the multi-decade highs that previously triggered verbal intervention.

Traders will focus on three catalysts: the policy statement, wage data out of Tokyo, and US payrolls on Friday. A break below 155.80 would open the door to a deeper retracement, while sustained trade above 158.00 puts the rally back in play.

Volatility has crept higher into the event window, so position sizing and stop placement matter more than usual.
