How this strategy was reviewed

The deck was walked through live with everyone below, then left open for a full week of written feedback in this studio.

    All 17 ideas presented one by one against the prototypes. Everyone invited saw the same screens at the same time.

  2. 2Day 0 · CallDiscussion and first reactions

    Open discussion on priorities, risks and open questions. Points raised were captured as comments on each idea.

  3. 3Day 1 – 7 · AsyncOne week for feedback

    Each person reviewed the prototypes at their own pace and left comments, questions and support votes.
